## Why it belongs

Black currant is adapted to cold winters and short seasons and supports a wide range of processed products. It is not intrinsically drought tolerant: shallow roots make dependable irrigation important through harvest. Its place in the catalog is as an efficient short-season irrigated crop, not a dry-farmed one.
