## Why it belongs

Chokecherry may be the strongest philosophic fit for the cold system: begin with a plant already adapted to difficult western environments, then select it toward a better agricultural product. It combines broad hardiness with potentially strong fruit yield, although productive irrigation remains higher than survival water.
