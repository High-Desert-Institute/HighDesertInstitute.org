## Why it belongs

Haskap combines extreme winter hardiness, frost-tolerant flowers, and very early fruit. Those traits directly address the short-season constraints of Mammoth- and Alturas-type environments. It needs dependable moisture, so its value lies in fitting a cold, short irrigation season rather than being xeric.
