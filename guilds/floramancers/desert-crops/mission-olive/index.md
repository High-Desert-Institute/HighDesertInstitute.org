## Why it belongs

Mission is among the more cold-tolerant traditional California olives. It can produce a concentrated, shelf-stable product and can be managed with regulated deficit irrigation, making it one of the strongest candidates for warm high-desert agriculture.

Hot/cold sites such as Bishop and Kingman should be treated as selection environments rather than assumed commercial orchard locations.
