## Why it belongs

Saskatoon is already treated as an agricultural crop in parts of Canada and is well adapted to northern and western climates. Its raw fruit yield per unit of irrigation may be lower than chokecherry, so product value, harvest efficiency, and cultivar quality are central research questions.
