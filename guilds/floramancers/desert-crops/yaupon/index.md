## Why it belongs

Yaupon may connect the hot and cold sides of this initiative. It tolerates heat and varied soils, has selected cultivars extending beyond the ordinary species hardiness range, and produces a distinctive caffeinated product. Its largest unresolved question is how much irrigation a repeatedly harvested leaf plantation needs in the arid West.

This makes yaupon both a prospective crop and a high-value research subject.
