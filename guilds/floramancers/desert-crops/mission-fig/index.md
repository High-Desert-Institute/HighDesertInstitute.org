## Why it belongs

Mission figs thrive under dry summer heat, but productive orchards use substantially more water than their reputation for drought survival suggests. They are a strong hot-desert crop and a useful breeding target where summer ripening conditions are excellent but winter cold damages ordinary Mission wood.
