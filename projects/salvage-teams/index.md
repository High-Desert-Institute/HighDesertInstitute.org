---

Salvage teams source materials for the builds and repairs that land project crews are working on. Crews share the quantities, dimensions, and condition they need, whether that’s enough reclaimed lumber for shelving or a set of matching doors and hinges. The teams check the [Recycling and Upcycling Depots](/projects/recycling-and-upcycling-depots/) first, then look to reuse stores, deconstruction crews, contractors with surplus stock, and community donors.

The teams check whether each find fits the job or whether the crew can adapt it. They arrange pickup and deliver usable materials to the crew or its depot.
