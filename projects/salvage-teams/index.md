Salvage teams start with the work each land project has planned. Crews share the quantities, dimensions, and condition they need, whether that’s enough reclaimed lumber for shelving or a set of matching doors and hinges. The teams check the [Recycling and Upcycling Depots](/projects/recycling-and-upcycling-depots/) first, then look to reuse stores, deconstruction crews, contractors with surplus stock, and community donors.

When a potential match turns up, the team confirms that it fits the job or can be adapted, then arranges pickup and delivery to the crew or its depot.
