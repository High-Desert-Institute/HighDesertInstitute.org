---

SalvageCorps will work with local businesses to recover usable discards, including from commercial dumpsters where we have permission. Fixtures, hardware, and other materials that can be reused or repaired will go to the [Recycling and Upcycling Depots](/projects/recycling-and-upcycling-depots/) for sorting and storage.

For food that will be shared with people, we’ll ask stores to set aside safe surplus for direct pickup. That gives volunteers and food-sharing partners a chance to verify the source and handling and maintain safe temperatures during transport.

We’ll ask cafés to set aside spent coffee grounds for regular pickup. We’ll bring the grounds to land projects, where the [Floramancers](/guilds/floramancers/) can mix them with dry leaves or other carbon-rich material to make compost that helps the soil retain water.
